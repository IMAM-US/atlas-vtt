# Places

Each place below is **declared**: this is what it stands for on this board. A place is only a meaning until its legend says what it stands for, and even then, being in it never acts. Moving a token between places changes this scene and nothing native.

## Herald's waystation
Where quests enter from outside this rig. The coordinator (`external-atlas-plan-coordinator`) is not a seat of `atlas-integration-repos`, so it stands at the border.

## Atlas Keep
The Atlas repository domain. The Atlas representative works in `/Users/farieds/Project/atlas-recorded-agent-scene`.

## OpenRig Foundry
The OpenRig repository domain. The OpenRig representative works in `/Users/farieds/Project/openrig`. The painted alcoves are decoration; the actual seats are the ones OpenRig reports.

## River and bridge
West is Atlas and east is OpenRig. The bridge is the integration worktree `/Users/farieds/Project/atlas-openrig-integration-plan`, where the planners and the image partner work.

## Planning Pavilion
The `planning` pod. In OpenRig a pod is a context domain, not a team rank or a workflow stage.

## Abandoned camp

## Painter's atelier
The image partner's domain: the project's `artwork/` folder, which it alone writes. The hut is new because the seat appeared for this task.

## Quest board
