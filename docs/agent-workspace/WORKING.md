## Confirmed owner direction

Make OpenRig's utility available through Atlas's tabletop/RPG world while preserving the freedom and dynamic behavior of OpenRig, Codex and Claude Code. Native work, teams, maps and tokens can all change extensively. The focus is the essential meaning they share and how their labeled elements connect, rather than a fixed roster, layout or one-token-per-agent model. The [revised brief](integration-planning/2026-10-18/ontology-brief.md) preserves the owner's correction.

The owner and their delegates can craft and continually revise maps, imagery, names, arrangement and interpretations. Agent authorship is part of the intended experience. An owner may delegate ongoing curation of a scene without approving every move. A native roster refresh alone has no authority to rearrange a board; an authorized curation operation can deliberately create, transform, regroup or remove its representations. Scenes, collections, rigs, pods, roles and projects need not map one-to-one.

Atlas owns spatial authoring, navigation and representation. OpenRig owns its seats, supported coordination/lifecycle and owned work. Codex and Claude Code retain native conversations, repository instructions, skill/tool discovery, approvals, model configuration, temporary subagents and continuity operations. Notes and repository sources retain their own authority for knowledge and decisions. Atlas adds no competing queue, execution engine or mandatory RPG workflow. An unfamiliar native capability must remain reachable even when Atlas has no dedicated control for it.

## Proposed shared understanding

People and agents act toward purposes, in situations, using resources, and create consequences that can be inspected. RPG characters, parties, quests, places, clues and encounters offer useful ways to express that meaning. They do not establish that every native seat is a character, every pod a party, or every queue row a quest. Use a small shared grammar and preserve each native system's actual types and continuity rules; do not introduce a universal entity database before exercising the model.

Ontology names what exists and what a relationship means. Topology connects those things and gives them a spatial expression or navigable route. Epistemology records the grounds for an account: its source, perspective, time, revision, evidence and attributed judgment. They meet in a statement such as: "Mira owns Import audit, according to this queue observation." Moving Mira's appearance changes the board; handing off the audit changes native ownership; an old observation changes what the board can responsibly claim.

A role is normally contextual participation, such as reviewing one undertaking while implementing another. A role may have an independently named note when people need to discuss its continuity. A seat, occupant, conversation, role, task, artifact and appearance retain their own identities. Neither a reused address nor a familiar portrait proves continuity.

If a recognized colleague must outlive a native seat, an optional persona/home note can retain its meaning and an attributed binding history. This is authored continuity, with explicit grounds for each association; it does not turn a fork into an exact resume. Direct native references remain sufficient where that extra identity is unnecessary. Scene aliases and exact historical conversation references stay available.

## What a meaningful label connects

| Question | Proposed rule |
| --- | --- |
| What am I looking at? | An editable local caption, image and appearance. Several appearances can depict one subject, and one appearance can summarize a declared group. Decorative objects need no compulsory semantic record. |
| What does it stand for? | An explicit source-qualified referent or authored concept. Explain whether it follows a continuing seat/current occupant, pins an exact conversation or revision, or summarizes a stated set. Names and coordinates never resolve identity by themselves. |
| Why is it here? | A contextual role or relationship with a defined meaning, scope and author/source. Nearness, containment and a drawn line acquire operational meaning only when that meaning is explicitly declared. |
| What is happening? | Sourced observations and authored interpretations remain distinguishable. A manual Working condition is an annotation; native activity comes from its actual observation. |
| How do we know? | Keep evidence, freshness, revision and attributed judgment accessible. Queue closure, an artifact's existence, acceptance of its revision, and owner adoption are different claims. |

Most detail belongs in an inspector or linked note. A compact visible label can say "Mira / reviewing Import audit" while the inspector resolves its subject, scope and evidence. The board and addressed text should expose the same meaning to humans and native agents. The detailed [labeling example and change rules](ontology-synthesis.md#labeling-through-one-concrete-example) make this proposal concrete. These questions guide design; they are not five mandatory fields on every object.

## The colleague journey and what stays native

1. Select an appearance in Atlas and inspect its meaning, current referent and useful context. A role-only or historical piece remains useful without pretending it is a live colleague.
2. Open the resolved native conversation through a supported entrance. Codex or Claude Code keeps its own interface, tools, skills, permissions and session mechanics. Exact visible per-seat opening still needs qualification; label an available team-terminal fallback honestly.
3. Ask conversationally or create durable owned work when appropriate. Atlas can offer a supported native operation with explicit targets and context. Drawing an arrow or placing a work card does not silently submit work.
4. Work continues natively, including temporary helpers, team growth, forks and handoffs. An authorized agent can reshape the investigation scene and publish useful context while native facts are reconciled independently of scene history.
5. Open the actual result and inspect its revision, evidence and judgments. Reuse OpenRig proof where configured; use attributed accounts elsewhere. A historical scene may retain an earlier accepted revision while a current scene shows changed evidence or disagreement.

The [full journey and stress cases](ontology-synthesis.md#journey-and-change-cases) cover shared subjects across scenes, changing group membership, forks/compaction, occupant replacement, renamed artwork and conflicting accounts. Closing Atlas stops none of the native work. Board undo reverses authored edits, not external messages, handoffs or lifecycle operations.

## Existing foundations and real gaps

Planning baseline: Atlas `f5dfe51`, OpenRig checkout `63db5917`, installed OpenRig 0.6.10 build `3001cc43`. The two revised reports inspect this planning cut. Source inspection, native operations and observed user acceptance remain separate evidence.

| Existing foundation | Integration gap |
| --- | --- |
| Editable scenes, tokens/characters, notes, encounters/formations, conditions, widgets, visibility, persistence and undo | Generic semantic references/interpretations, useful relations and scoped agent-readable/editable scene operations with conflict handling. Game presets do not already provide an agent ontology. |
| Immutable recorded seat/work/artifact references and inspectors | A distinct current-reference model and live resolver. Preserve historical references; do not silently reinterpret them as live bindings. |
| Prepared vault-home discovery and bounded caretaker evidence | General addressed context and dynamic board authorship. Homes are optional contextual destinations, not a required role-to-seat registry. |
| OpenRig inventory, native terminals, queue, continuity, context and events | Current shared projection, supported native entrances/actions, freshness and reconciliation after outside changes. Event coverage alone is insufficient. |
| Native artifacts and configured revision-sensitive proof judgments | Show the actual evidence and judgment authority. General hypotheses, meaning disputes and owner choices still need attributed accounts rather than fabricated proof receipts. |

RPG affordances retain their actual semantics: formations arrange local tokens, clocks express explicitly defined measures, fog controls presentation, and initiative orders tabletop attention. They do not by themselves launch teams, prove work, enforce access or schedule native agents. The [detailed gap map](ontology-synthesis.md#existing-features-and-integration-sequence) connects these boundaries to source evidence.

## Proposed integration sequence

Recommendations for later authorized work:

| Step | Outcome to demonstrate |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Exercise the shared meaning | Author one colleague/work/result example with two appearances and explicit current versus historical interpretations. A human and a native agent recover the same subjects, relations and evidence through the board and addressed text. Settle only the minimal semantics this example needs. |
| 2. Complete the native journey | Resolve current identity, open a qualified native entrance, ask/assign work with context, and inspect its actual result and judgment. Reconcile outside changes, reused names, stale observations and changed evidence. |
| 3. Support dynamic authorship | Delegate one investigation scene, including creation, transformation, regrouping and removal of appearances. Handle concurrent human edits and keep native operations separate from reversible board edits. Demonstrate a useful aggregate without forcing every helper into a seat or token. |
| 4. Generalize exercised needs | Extend relationships, reusable vocabularies, cross-scene/project/host references and qualified native controls. Preserve unknown native extensions and optional workflows. No prescribed layout or second orchestration system. |

This order replaces the earlier resource-launcher-first framing. Engineering should use deterministic source resolution and operation adapters; existing agents interpret and curate meaning under the owner's delegation. The ontology is a shared understanding to prove through use, not an additional execution policy.
