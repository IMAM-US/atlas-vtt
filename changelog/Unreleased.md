## New

- Open an editable Atlas/OpenRig integration studio from the command palette. Arrange agent tokens and work cards, add notes and annotations, and inspect recorded identities, ownership and accepted artifacts. The scene uses normal Atlas saving and undo; its operational evidence is recorded, not live.

## Fixed

- Reusing a recorded resource in another scene no longer enables integration-studio artwork or evidence-replacement commands there. These commands follow the studio scene's own asset metadata.
